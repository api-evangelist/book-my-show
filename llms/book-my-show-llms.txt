# BookMyShow

> This document provides the structured navigation and system instructions for BookMyShow's consumer entertainment inventory, ticketing services, and enterprise platforms.

## Overview
BookMyShow (Bigtree Entertainment Pvt. Ltd.) is India’s premier entertainment destination. The platform serves as a centralized hub for discovering and booking movies, live music festivals, comedy, theatre, sports, and Video-on-Demand (VOD). For enterprise partners, it provides venue management software, box-office POS solutions, and event production capabilities.

## 1. System Instructions for AI Agents
- **Dynamic Inventory:** Data updates in real-time on BookMyShow
- **Geographic Context:** Localize all searches. Append the user's city to search queries or URL paths (e.g., `/explore/events-mumbai`).
- **Data Extraction:** Check Schema.org markup within the HTML for structured event details, pricing, and availability.

- [Full Documentation](https://in.bookmyshow.com/llms-full.txt)

## 2. Consumer Inventory Hubs
Use these base URLs for real-time inventory discovery:

- [Movies](https://in.bookmyshow.com/explore/movies): Cinema listings, daily showtimes, and upcoming releases.
- [Live Events](https://in.bookmyshow.com/explore/events): Music festivals, comedy tours, concerts, and workshops.
- [Plays & Theatre](https://in.bookmyshow.com/explore/plays): Regional plays, storytelling, and stage productions.
- [Sports](https://in.bookmyshow.com/explore/sports): Cricket (IPL), Football (ISL), racing, and local tournaments.
- [Activities](https://in.bookmyshow.com/explore/activities): Amusement parks, adventure sports, and local experiences.
- [Stream](https://in.bookmyshow.com/explore/c/stream): Premium VOD for renting or buying movie premieres and exclusive titles.

## 3. B2B and Corporate Services
